# Unscroll privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026.

Unscroll is a home screen app for Android with focus tools. This policy explains what it does with
information on your phone.

## Summary

Unscroll has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It does not collect, sell or
share any personal information. What you set up stays on your phone.

## What Unscroll stores on your phone

- Your home pages: the apps on each page, names you gave them, and the theme.
- Your focus settings: the apps you limit, Block now's apps and websites, your schedules and options.
- Running sessions: which limited app you chose time for, and until when.

These are kept in Unscroll's private app storage, are not backed up by Unscroll, and are deleted when
you uninstall Unscroll.

## The focus guard (Accessibility service)

If you turn on the focus guard, Unscroll uses Android's Accessibility service to:

- know the name of the app in front, so it can show the time question, Time's up, or Blocked for the
  apps you chose;
- while you block websites, read the address shown in a supported browser's address bar, so it can
  show Blocked for the sites you chose.

Unscroll uses this information at once to decide what to show, and does not store it, log it or send
it anywhere. It does not read other screen content such as messages or passwords. You can turn the
focus guard off at any time in Android's Accessibility settings.

## Permissions

- **Accessibility service**: only if you turn on the focus guard, as described above.
- **Set wallpaper**: only when you choose "Save matching wallpaper", to set a plain wallpaper.
- **Seeing installed apps**: to list your apps on the home screen and in the app pickers. The list is
  not stored except for the apps you choose.

## Links out of the app

Contact support opens a GitHub issues page, and Rate Unscroll opens Google Play, in your
browser or store app. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Children

Unscroll is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective
date.
